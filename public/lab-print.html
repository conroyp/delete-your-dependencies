WHAT THE PAGE SENDS

Four blank lines, the attached mark, the three greeting lines, then six blank lines for clean spacing.

The mark is converted to black-and-white raster data before it is sent.

ON STAGE

A direct print.

Choose a device, connect to its writable GATT characteristic, and send the image and text as printer bytes. No server or print dialog is involved.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bluetooth printers do not share one universal BLE service or print protocol. The defaults are a common ESC/POS-style profile; enter the service and characteristic UUIDs for your printer if they differ. Classic Bluetooth-only printers will not appear as GATT devices.
